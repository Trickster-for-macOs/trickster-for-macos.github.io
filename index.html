How It Works

Step 1

Install and launch Trickster. It begins tracking files you open, edit, or download automatically.

Step 2

Click the Trickster icon in your menu bar anytime to view your recent files, sorted by recency.

Step 3

Hover to preview, right-click for actions like open, move, or share. Files respond instantly.

Step 4

Customize via preferences: adjust list size, exclude apps, or pin favorites for always-ready access.
